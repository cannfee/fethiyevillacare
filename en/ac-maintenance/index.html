Frequently Asked Questions

How often should an AC be cleaned?

It depends on usage, surroundings and manufacturer guidance. Check filters at the intervals in your unit manual.

Can a dirty AC cause an odour?

Dirt on filters or accessible areas can contribute to odours. If the smell continues, a technician may need to check for another issue.

Does cleaning reduce energy use?

Clean filters can support airflow, but energy use depends on the unit and many usage conditions. If you cannot notice an improvement in airflow and energy use after cleaning, we refund the cleaning fee; we explain the terms before booking.

Is refrigerant included?

No. Refrigerant checks or work are not assumed to be part of standard cleaning and should be discussed separately.

How do I book in Fethiye?

Send your unit capacity and location on WhatsApp so we can confirm availability and current pricing.

How long does AC cleaning take?

Timing depends on the number of units, access and condition. Share the number and capacity of your units when requesting an appointment.

Are the outdoor unit and refrigerant included?

The brochure prices should not be taken to include these tasks. Ask for separate scope and pricing if they are needed.

Will cleaning repair a faulty air conditioner?

Cleaning is not a technical repair. If the unit does not run, leaks water or continues to perform poorly, a technician may need to assess it separately.

Does cleaning prevent allergies or illness?

No health outcome can be guaranteed. Cleaning can help remove dust and dirt from filters and support suitable airflow.
