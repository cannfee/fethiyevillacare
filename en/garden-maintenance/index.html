Work to consider for a villa garden

A one-off or recurring plan can be prepared after considering the garden size, plants and current condition:

  • Lawn mowing and edging
  • Collecting weeds, leaves and plant debris
  • Basic care for planting beds, ornamental plants and shrubs
  • Visual checks of irrigation heads where offered and suitable

Seasonal care and irrigation

During Fethiye’s hot, dry periods, watering needs vary by plant, soil and shade. Tell us in advance if you need a timer or drip-line check; technical repairs and replacement parts should not be assumed to be included.

Pruning, green waste and safe-work limits

The scope for shrubs and accessible plants is confirmed after assessing the garden. Tall trees, branches near power lines, climbing or chainsaw work require specific skills and equipment; do not assume these are offered unless confirmed. Collection and removal of green waste should also be stated in the quote.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does garden care include?

Lawn, plant, weed and general clean-up work can be planned around your needs. We list the confirmed work in the quote.

How often should a garden be maintained?

It depends on the garden, season and irrigation. We can discuss a suitable visit schedule during the first conversation.

Do you trim trees?

It depends on the tree, size and location. Tall trees, branches near power lines or work requiring specialist equipment need separate assessment.

Is green waste removal included?

Collection and removal depend on the agreed scope and should be confirmed before the appointment.

How is the price determined?

We consider garden size and condition, requested work, visit frequency and materials or waste removal.

Can lawn mowing and pruning be done in one visit?

They can be planned together depending on the garden and requested work. Confirm the tasks and expected scope before booking.

Do you provide tree pruning or pest treatment?

High-risk pruning and chemical treatment should not be assumed unless the required qualifications and local requirements are confirmed. Send photos and ask what can be arranged.

Is green waste taken away?

Collection, transport and disposal depend on the agreed scope. Confirm this before the appointment.

Should I send photos of the garden?

Yes. Photos along with an approximate size, location and task list can make an initial WhatsApp quote assessment easier.
