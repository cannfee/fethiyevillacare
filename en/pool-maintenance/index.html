Care based on your pool’s needs

Surface debris removal, suitable cleaning of the pool floor and walls, a general assessment of the water and checks of accessible equipment can be planned according to need.

Water balance and filtration

Where the system and agreed scope allow, pH or chlorine checks, filter pressure observation and an assessment against manufacturer guidance may be included. Chemical products and work are discussed before the visit.

One-off or recurring visits

You can ask about a pre-season visit, a clean after heavy use or recurring care. Frequency depends on pool size, use, weather and equipment.

Request a pool quote

Quotes depend on location, approximate dimensions or volume, current condition, visit frequency and materials. A pool photo on WhatsApp can help with an initial assessment.

Clear water, peace of mind

Clear-looking water alone does not confirm that a pool is balanced and ready to use. When care is missed, water balance can shift and cloudiness, odours or unwanted microorganism growth may become more likely. We assess cleaning, water checks and filtration around your pool.

Free site visit and a clear quote

We assess the pool size, current condition and your needs at no charge in Fethiye and nearby areas. You receive a clear quote covering the work, visit frequency and any materials before we begin. Send a photo on WhatsApp to get started.

Physical cleaning for a villa pool

Possible work is assessed against the pool size, surface and condition before it is included in a quote:

  • Removing leaves and debris from the water surface
  • Cleaning the pool floor and walls with suitable equipment
  • Cleaning accessible areas such as the waterline and skimmer basket
  • Discussing vacuum cleaning and a general visual check when requested

Water balance, filters and equipment

Where the system allows, pH and free chlorine checks, filter pressure observation and an assessment against manufacturer guidance can be discussed. Backwashing, chemical dosing and consumables are not the same for every pool, so confirm them explicitly in the quote.

Maintenance frequency and safe use

Visit frequency depends on use, weather, nearby vegetation, pool volume and filtration. Chemicals should only be handled according to measurements and product labels; chlorine products must not be mixed with acids. A pool should not be described as ready for use before its water conditions are assessed.

How the service is arranged

  1. Send your request and location on WhatsApp.

  3. Confirm the service scope and quote.
  4. Choose a suitable appointment time together.
  5. Complete the agreed work and review what was done.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does pool maintenance include?

Depending on need, it may include physical cleaning, a water condition assessment and checks of accessible filtration equipment. We confirm the exact scope before quoting.

Do you check pH and chlorine?

Checks can be discussed where the system and agreed service allow. We confirm measurements and chemical work in advance.

Are pool chemicals included?

Materials and chemicals depend on the pool and requested work; the quote will state what is included.

Can you help with cloudy or green water?

We first assess the water and equipment. Any work and readiness for use should be determined after appropriate checks.

How is the price determined?

Pool size, condition, location, visit frequency and materials are considered. You can send a photo on WhatsApp.

Can I arrange weekly or fortnightly visits?

Tell us the schedule you have in mind. We can quote a suitable frequency after checking the pool and service location.

Can a green pool be used straight away?

No. The water and equipment need to be assessed first. Any treatment and readiness for use should be determined after appropriate checks.

Do you repair pumps or leaks?

Electrical, pump and structural repairs are not assumed to be part of routine care. If an issue is noticed, it can be reported and a qualified technician may be needed.

Are chemicals and consumables included?

That depends on the pool and requested work. The quote should state which products and materials are included.
